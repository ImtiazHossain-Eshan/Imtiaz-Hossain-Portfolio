## The product argument

A property portfolio is not one number. It is gross rent falling through operating
costs and tax to whatever actually survives. Portico makes that subtraction visible
on every property and across the whole register, pairing owner-scoped records with a
financial engine that **recomputes return, yield, cap rate, and cashflow from source
transactions** rather than storing impressive-looking percentages.

The gross-to-net waterfall is the product's core component, and it appears at four
scales: dashboard summary, property detail, tax module, and gross-vs-net report. Each
deduction shows a bar proportional to gross and its share as a percentage. Every figure
is derived by `domain/Finance.kt`, so a newly added property is analysed by exactly the
same rules as the seeded ones.

## Multi-currency, by design

A portfolio spanning Dhaka, Montevideo, and Buenos Aires holds three currencies.
Conversion happens **once**, at the boundary of `Finance.analyse`, so everything
downstream works in a single currency without knowing conversion exists. Rates are
expressed as units per 1 USD and are user assumptions, not a feed: the same philosophy
as the tax rates. Switching display currency moves every amount and leaves every ratio
untouched; ROI, cap rate, and net yield come out identical in taka or dollars.

## Portico Intelligence

The analysis assistant answers portfolio questions **on-device by default**. Cloud
analysis via Gemma is opt-in and sends only computed figures, never an address or a
document. Every answer carries the arithmetic that produced it, so a figure can be
checked rather than trusted.

## Tax, payments, and portability

Tax rules for nine jurisdictions across Bangladesh, Uruguay, and Argentina ship as
**editable assumptions**, each carrying its own working (e.g., `0.75 × 12% = 9% of
gross rent`). Two checkout routes run in sandbox mode: a built-in server-verified flow
with Luhn validation and distinct decline paths, and an SSLCommerz gateway for real
sandbox transactions. CSV export and import support duplicate detection and line-level
rejection reporting.

## Adaptive interface and Bangla

Navigation is structural: a bottom bar on a phone, a rail from 600 dp, and a wider
working surface with three-column metrics from 840 dp. The platform ships in English and
Bangla across **770 translated strings**, switchable in-app or from Android's per-app
language settings.
